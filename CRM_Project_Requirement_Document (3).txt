1. Purpose and Goals
Build a role-based CRM to capture, assign, follow up, and convert leads (admissions/education-style flow: course,
qualification, country). It must match the core experience of Zoho CRM: powerful filters, saved views, lead timeline,
email/notes/call from inside the lead, pipeline, automations and reports.

Primary goals
• One place to manage all leads with fast filtering and saved views.
• Strict hierarchy-based access (Counselor, Team Lead, Manager, Admin, Super Admin).
• Full activity history (timeline) for every lead.
• Click-to-call through the IVR dialer, scheduled-call popups, and in-app email.
• Duplicate prevention with controlled visibility and merging.
• Downloadable reports built from lead filters.

Saved filters
• User can name and save any filter combination.
• Visibility rule: a saved filter belongs to its creator and is visible to the creator's superiors (Team Lead,
Manager, Admin, Super Admin above them). It is not visible to peer counselors or other users outside that
chain.

• Saved filters can be edited, renamed, deleted by the owner, and pinned to the Dashboard.
Lead table (right side)
• Column chooser: Name, Phone, Email, Country, Course, Qualification, Lead Stage, Assignee, Next Follow-up,
Created On, Modified On, etc.

• Column selection saved per user.
• Sorting, pagination, bulk select (bulk assign, bulk stage change, export).
• Click a row to open the Lead Detail page.

4.3 Create New Lead
• "Create Lead" button on the Leads page.
• Fields: Name, Phone, Alternate Mobile, Email, Country, Course, Qualification, Lead Stage, Source, Assignee,
Next Follow-up.

• Duplicate check (on phone and email, real time): if a match exists, show a duplicate warning and the name
of the user the lead belongs to.

• Counselor / Team Lead: "View Lead" shows only the assignee name (no lead details).
• Manager / Admin / Super Admin: "View Lead" opens the complete lead detail page.
• Save is blocked for exact duplicates (configurable by Admin).

Header fields: Name, Phone (click-to-call), Email, Lead Stage, Follow-up date and time, Course, Country,

Email section (sidebar)
• Opens the logged-in user's (Counselor / Team Lead / Manager) Gmail template by default.
• User types the message in-app and clicks Send; the mail is delivered to the email address stored on that lead.
• Sent mail is logged in the timeline (subject, time, sender).
Notes section (sidebar)
• Add a note; saved with user name, date and time.
• Notes listed newest first and mirrored in the timeline.
Timeline tab (available in Overview, Email and Notes)
• Records every activity with date and time: lead created, source, assigned to whom, reassigned, field edits,
stage changes, calls (with duration/outcome), scheduled calls, emails, notes, merges.

• Read-only and non-deletable (audit trail).

4.5 Click-to-Call (IVR Dialer)
• Clicking the phone number triggers the IVR provider API to start the call from the user's registered
extension/agent number.

• The call appears automatically in the dialer screen (no manual dialing).
• Call log (start, end, duration, status, recording link if provided) is stored and added to the timeline.
• Inbound/webhook events update the lead activity.

4.6 Schedule a Call and Popup Notifications
• User picks date and time on the lead; the call is saved against that lead and user.
• At the scheduled time, the user who scheduled it (Counselor, Team Lead, Manager, Admin) gets an in-app
popup and notification with lead name, phone and a Call Now button.

• Optional reminder 10 minutes before. Options: Snooze, Mark done, Reschedule.
• Delivery in real time, with browser notification as backup.

4.7 View and Merge Leads
• Anyone can see who a lead is assigned to when a duplicate is found.
• Merge rules depend on role:
Role

Can merge when

Counselor

Both leads belong to the counselor

Team Lead

Both leads belong to the Team Lead or their team

Manager

Both leads are within the manager's branch

Admin / Super Admin

Any two leads

• If both leads are outside the user's scope, the user can only see the owner, not merge.
• Merge flow: choose master record, choose which field values to keep, confirm. Timelines of both leads are
combined and the secondary lead is archived, not hard-deleted.

4.8 Pipeline
• Kanban board by lead stage (e.g., New, Contacted, Interested, Application, Closed Won, Closed Lost).
• Drag and drop to change stage (logged in timeline).
• Card shows name, course, country, follow-up, assignee. Respects saved filters.

4.9 Automations
• Trigger, condition, action rules.
• Examples: round-robin assignment of new leads, auto follow-up date when stage changes, reminder if no
activity in 2 days, escalate to Team Lead if follow-up is overdue.

• Enable/disable rules, execution log.

8. Non-Functional Requirements
• Security: password hashing, token-based sessions with refresh, role checks on every API (not just UI),
encrypted integration secrets, HTTPS.

• Performance: lead list under 2 seconds for 100k+ leads (server-side filtering and pagination, indexes).
• Audit: all data changes, merges and downloads logged.
• Availability: daily database backups.
• Usability: responsive layout; desktop-first.

10. Acceptance Criteria (Samples)
1. A counselor cannot see leads or saved filters of another counselor.
2. A filter saved by a counselor is visible to that counselor and their Team Lead, Manager, Admin and Super
Admin, but not to peers.

3. Entering an existing phone or email shows the duplicate warning and owner name; counselors and team leads
see only the owner name, managers and above can open the full lead.

4. Every lead action (create, assign, edit, call, email, note, merge) appears in the timeline with date and time.
5. Clicking the phone number starts the call on the IVR dialer automatically.
6. A scheduled call triggers a popup at the exact time for the user who scheduled it.
7. Merge is allowed only per the role rules in section 4.7.
8. Reports download with exactly the filtered data the user is allowed to see.
9. Only Super Admin can create, delete, activate, deactivate users and reset passwords.
10. A website contact form submission creates a lead within 5 seconds, tagged with its source, and assigns it by
the active assignment rule.

11. A new WhatsApp message from an unknown number and a completed chatbot conversation each create a
lead with the conversation stored in the timeline.

12. A repeat enquiry from an existing phone or email does not create a duplicate; it adds a timeline entry and
notifies the lead owner.

13. Clicking a phone number starts the call through the third-party IVR with no manual dialing, and the call log
appears in the timeline automatically.

14. A user can schedule a Zoom meeting from a lead, the lead receives the join link by email, and the meeting
appears in the timeline.

15. The interface uses the navy and white palette and Inter typography defined in section 12.
16. Saving an invoice with no discount emails it to the lead immediately, with the Razorpay QR for the full
amount.

17. Saving an invoice with a discount sends nothing until an Admin or Super Admin approves; after approval the
invoice is emailed with a Razorpay QR for the discounted amount.

18. A completed Razorpay payment marks the invoice as Paid, updates the lead's closure fee and adds a
timeline entry.

14. IVR Click-to-Call Integration (Third-Party)
The CRM connects to the company's third-party IVR provider through an adapter layer, so the provider can be
changed without touching the UI. Clicking a phone number places the call automatically.

14.1 Call Flow
1. The user clicks the phone number on the lead.
2. The Next.js app calls the ivr-click-to-call Edge Function with the lead id.
3. The function finds the user's IVR agent id or extension and calls the provider's click-to-call API.
4. The provider rings the agent's dialer first, then connects the lead. The call shows on the dialer automatically
with no manual dialing.

5. The provider sends webhooks (ringing, answered, ended, recording ready) to the ivr-webhook Edge Function,
which verifies the signature.

6. The function saves the call in the calls table, logs the event in ivr_events and writes the timeline entry.
7. When the call ends, a popup asks for the outcome, a note and the next follow-up date.

16.4 Razorpay QR and Payment
• The QR is created with the Razorpay QR Code API for the exact final amount, with an expiry date, and carries
the invoice id and lead id in its notes.

• The QR image is placed in the email and on the invoice PDF.
• A Razorpay webhook (signature verified) updates the invoice to Paid, saves the payment in the payments table,
updates the lead's closure fee, notifies the counselor and sends a payment receipt email.

• If the QR expires, the user clicks Resend to create a new QR and email it again.
• Razorpay keys are stored as Supabase secrets, never in the frontend.
